FIP in Cats

FIP is a serious disease in cats caused by a changed form of a common cat coronavirus. It used to be almost always fatal. Today there is treatment that works. My cat Mazie had FIP, and it got so bad that she was paralyzed. She is now healed and doing great. She turned 1 on June 25.
